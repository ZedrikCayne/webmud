Welcome to webmud Created because I needed to dog-food a web server. Commands you can type in: /info Returns the basic info. How many incoming connections are connected to your worlds (you can connect from more than one browser at a time. They all display the same live stuff. /connect <name> <address> <port> [ssl | sslv1] Connect to a remote socket. Will do SSL (and old, insecure ssl if the mud server you are talking to doesn't get modern ssl.) If you are on a disonnected connection, giving no parameters will cause it to reconnect. /next Switch to the next connection /prev Switch to the last connection /pick Select your world by number /disconnect Disconnect your current connection. Keeps your backscroll and you can /connect again. /kill Disconnect your current connection and get rid of the backscroll. /kick Disconnect all the other browsers. Just kills the websockets. /recall Redisplay the backscroll for whatever connection you are on.
