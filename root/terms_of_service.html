Welcome to webmud This is running on an absurdly small machine as a practical test for this teeny tiny webserver. It is a reasonably servicable mud client. And while we do hold a reasonably large backscroll, do not depend on it being here as it is completely ephemeral. One good power glitch and it's gone. Expect down time as I'm messing with it when I have a spare minute or three.
