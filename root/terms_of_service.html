Welcome to webmud It is a reasonably servicable mud client. And while we do hold a reasonably large backscroll, do not depend on it being here as it is completely ephemeral. One good power glitch and it's gone. We do log where you are coming from and what pages you access. Sorry, it's the only way to shut down folks who knock on the front door of the web server with ill intent. Expect down time as I'm messing with it when I have a spare minute or three. We restrict the output ports you can try to connect to as well as any addresses on a local network. (A local network address will connect from the server's point of view to *itself* if you ask for localhost for example...sorry, not going to allow that.) Output connections are only ready for ipv4, not ipv6. Might do that in the future if things get hosted on a 'real' network. We reserve the right to block anyone who tries to use the server for evil. No plans for world domination, pushing old ladies down the stairs etc. And please, do not use this for emergency contact, 911 services, etc. Use your head.
